## 3. Key findings

1. **Posters are dead weight.** 12 static posters = 50% of owned volume but only ~8% of interactions (45 of 537). Average poster reached **203 people (~8% of followers)** — Instagram's algorithm barely distributes static posts. Posters drew **0 comments and 0 shares across all 12.**
2. **Reels are the only format that works.** Avg reel reached **1,237 (52% of followers, often >100%)** — i.e. reels are the only thing escaping the follower bubble. They carry essentially all the shares and saves.
3. **The account itself is the ceiling.** Even the single best post reached **2,711** — barely above follower count. Effort is being spent on a 2.4K-follower handle while the 38.9K main account sat idle for this content.
4. **Conversation is near-zero.** 12 reels → 14 comments total; 12 posters → 0. Low community signal.
5. **Influencer spend is unmeasured.** 5 influencer reels delivered with no performance reporting — we paid for reach we can't see.

## 6. Recommendations for the next agency

1. **Kill or slash static posters** — or convert them to carousels/motion. Flat images don't distribute.
2. **Reels-first mandate** — make reels the core deliverable; set a minimum (e.g. 8–10/mo) with hook + retention requirements.
3. **Decide the account strategy** — either grow `@filli_tn` deliberately (follower growth as a KPI) or post hero content to the main `@fillicafe` for real reach. Don't spend on a 2.4K handle by default.
4. **Mandatory influencer reporting** — every paid influencer piece must come with reach/views/saves, ideally via screenshot + their insights export.
5. **Set baseline KPIs** so the new agency is accountable: avg reel reach, ER on reach (target >4%), saves+shares per reel, follower growth/mo, and ≥1 measurable CTA per month.
6. **If you share the Hashtag retainer fee**, I can compute cost per reel / per 1,000 reach / per interaction to quantify ROI.
